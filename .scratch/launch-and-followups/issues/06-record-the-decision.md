# 06: Record the three-week decision

**What to build:** The decision the launch exists to produce, written down with its numbers. The signal table in the decision memo is filled from observation — installs, issues, forks, the first complaint — and one outcome is chosen: continue, narrow further, park, or pivot to evals. A park is recorded honestly, with a post-mortem paragraph rather than silence.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Publish the launch artifact and open the signal window) plus three calendar weeks.

**Status:** human-gated — a person decides; the agent can gather and tabulate the signals.

- [ ] The signal table is filled: installs, stars, forks, external issues, first complaint, false-positive reports
- [ ] One outcome is chosen and the numbers that decided it are quoted
- [ ] If the outcome is park: the limits page gets a post-mortem paragraph and the README says the project is unmaintained
- [ ] If the outcome is continue: the first complaint becomes the next ticket, ahead of any new corpus work
- [ ] If the outcome is pivot: the named asset (committed-test replay and the integrity taxonomy) and the proposed home are recorded
